Nature words and useful rules 📚

Tap a card to hear a British English voice. Use Slow audio if you need more time.

Comparatives ⚖️

Compare two things: adjective + -er + than.

wide → wider · big → bigger · dry → drier

The lake is deeper than the stream.

Superlatives 🥇

Choose number one: the + adjective + -est.

high → the highest · big → the biggest · dry → the driest

The mountain is the highest place.

Long adjectives 🌟

Use more… than for a comparison and the most… for number one.

This path is more interesting than that path.

Everyday English 🎟️

“I’d like one child ticket, please.”

“Would you like to pay by cash or card?”

“By card, please.”
